The short version

  • You can play against the computer as a guest with everything stored on your device, or sign in with Google to play online, add friends, join the café and compete in the rankings.
  • We never ask for your photos, contacts, camera, microphone or location. Chat (phrases, emojis, short messages and stickers) is screened by an automatic filter, relayed live, and never written to our database — a message another player reports is the only exception.
  • Coins, gems and tickets are virtual items with no real-world value. The app sells nothing for real money and offers no real-money gambling.
  • The game is free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob, with a consent screen where the law requires one.
  • You can delete your account at any time from Settings, with the form on the Data Deletion page, or by e-mail.
