You can delete your Domino Lux Stars account and the data linked to it at any time. There are three ways: instantly inside the app, with the request form on this page, or by e-mail. This page also lists exactly what is erased, what is kept for a short time, and how to delete only part of your data.

1. Delete your account in the app

This is the fastest way. It works while you are signed in to the account you want to delete.

  1. Open Domino Lux Stars and go to Settings (the gear icon).
  2. In the Account section, tap Delete Account.
  3. Read the warning and confirm. A match you are playing has to end first.
  4. Your sign-in record, your profile and all the data listed in section 3 are erased immediately and permanently from our servers, and the app signs you out. You can keep playing as a guest or create a new account later.
This cannot be undone. Coins, gems, tickets, cosmetics, friends, rankings and all progress are lost permanently.

If your account is banned, the app shows the ban screen instead of the lobby. That screen has a Request account deletion link to this page: use the form or the e-mail below and we will delete the account the same way.

2. Request deletion with the form or by e-mail

If you no longer have the app, cannot sign in, or prefer to ask us, use this form. Give the e-mail address of the Google account you signed in with, so we can verify it is really you, and your 8-digit player code (shown on your profile) if you know it.

Or write to userconplay@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Domino Lux Stars account”, from the Google address you used to sign in (or with your player code). Tell us whether you want the whole account deleted, or only specific data (see section 5).

We confirm receipt, carry out the deletion, and send you a confirmation e-mail — normally within a few days and always within 30 days.

5. Deleting only some data

You do not have to delete the whole account to remove specific data:

  • Friends and requests: remove a friend or cancel a request from the Friends page or the player’s card.
  • Blocked players: unblock them from Settings → Blocked players, or from the player’s card.
  • Presence, profile and watching: hide them in Privacy controls.
  • Push notification token: turn notifications off in Settings, or sign out.
  • Profile details: change your username, avatar, frame or country in your profile’s editor, and the language in Settings.
  • Anything else: use the form or e-mail us as in section 2 and say which data you want removed. We will do so unless we need it for the reasons in section 4.

6. Guest mode

If you play as a guest without signing in, none of your game data is on our servers — only a support request, if you send one (the name, e-mail address and message you typed). Use the form or e-mail us from that address to have it deleted; otherwise, once it is answered or closed, it is deleted automatically when it is 180 days old. Everything else (name, avatar, offline coins, settings, offline progress) is stored only on your phone. To delete it, clear the app’s storage in your device settings or uninstall the app. Ads, usage analytics and crash reports also work in guest mode; they are tied to your device, not to an account (see section 4).

7. Common questions

Can I get my account back after deleting it?

No. Deletion is permanent. Signing in again with the same Google account creates a brand-new, empty profile.

Will my coins, gems or tickets be refunded?

No. They are virtual items with no monetary value (see the Terms of Use).

I deleted the app. Is my account deleted too?

No. Uninstalling only removes local data. Your online profile stays until you delete it in the app, with the form, or by e-mail.

Someone else is using my account.

Write to us immediately from your Google address; we can delete or lock the account for you.

How do I remove my data from Google (ads, analytics)?

That data is controlled by Google (and, for ads, by its partner Unity Ads) under their own policies. Read Google’s Privacy Policy and learn how to reset or delete your Advertising ID.
