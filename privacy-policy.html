1. Guest mode and online mode

The App can be used in two ways, and the data involved is very different:

  • Guest (local) mode. No account is created. You play against computer players; your chosen name, avatar, offline coins, settings and offline progress are stored only on your device, and nothing about your play is sent to our game servers. The one exception is a support request you choose to send us (see 2.6 Support requests). Guest mode still shows ads (see Advertising) and sends usage analytics and crash reports (see Analytics and crash reports). Uninstalling the App erases this data.
  • Online mode. You sign in with your Google account — through Google Play services or your browser — and you can later sign back in to that account with a one-time code sent to its e-mail address. We create a player profile on our servers so you can play online matches, add friends, sit at the café, send gifts, and compete in the weekly ranking, the world ranking, the season pass and the elite challenge. The rest of this policy describes online mode unless stated otherwise.

2.2 Game progress and economy

  • Coins, gems and tickets, level and experience points, weekly ranking points and last week’s rank, world ranking points, season pass progress, elite challenge progress, daily check-ins, missions, chests and achievements.
  • Statistics for each of the six games: matches played, wins, losses, rounds, dominoes, blocked games, points scored and conceded, tiles placed and drawn, and similar records shown on your profile.
  • Items you own or have equipped (avatars, frames, tables, stone sets, backdrops), the gifts in your bag, and your virtual store purchases (what you bought, when, and its price in coins, gems or tickets).
  • Your popularity: the hearts that players who watched your matches sent you.
  • Match records, settled by our game server: the date, the game and its length, the stage, the players at the table, the entry fee, the result and what it paid (coins, experience, gems). They are used to settle the match, keep the rankings fair and answer support requests; the App shows no match history.
  • Café records: the café tables you sit at and the invitations you send or accept while a sitting is open. Café chat messages are kept only in the game server’s memory — the last 40 lines of each hall, gone when the sitting ends or the server restarts — and are never written to our database, except that a message another player reports is copied into that report so we can review it.
  • If you break the chat rules, the time until which your chat is muted, and — when our team mutes you — the reason. Automatic strikes from the chat filter are counted and forgotten one day after the last one.

2.3 Social features

  • Your friends list, friend requests you send and receive, private-room invitations, “remind me” requests to a friend at a table, and the list of players you have blocked.
  • Presence: whether you are online or playing and when you were last seen, so your friends can see if you are available. You can hide it in Privacy controls.
  • Watching: your friends can watch your online matches live and send hearts while they watch. You can turn watching off in Privacy controls.
  • Chat at the table: during an online match you can send preset phrases, emojis, short typed messages (60 characters at most, where typed chat is open) and stickers to the players at your table. A typed message passes an automatic filter before delivery; one that fails is not delivered and counts toward an automatic mute. Messages are relayed live by our game server to that table only, are rate-limited, and are not stored after delivery. You can mute any player, and a blocked player’s messages never reach you. Stickers and table gifts cost their price in gems or come from your gift bag.
  • Reports you send about a player: your account, the reported player, the reason, any note you add (300 characters at most), and — for a café message — the reported line.

2.4 Device and technical information

  • Push notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging) and your language, only if you turn notifications on.
  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics): app version, device model, operating system version and a crash trace, together with your random account identifier in online mode and, when a sign-in fails, which step failed and the version of Google Play services.
  • Usage analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase): automatically collected events such as app opens and session length, and a few game events (signing in, a match started or ended, virtual currency earned or spent), linked to your random account identifier in online mode — never to your e-mail address.
  • Advertising ID of your device, used by Google AdMob and its mediation partner Unity Ads to serve and measure ads (see Advertising).
  • App and device integrity verdicts from the Google Play Integrity API (see Security and anti-cheat).
  • IP address and connection data, processed transiently by the servers the App talks to (our database and authentication provider, our game server, and Google services) to deliver the service and protect it from abuse. A guest’s support request keeps a salted one-way hash of the address only, to limit the number of requests.

2.5 Data stored only on your device

Sound, music, vibration, language and notification settings, your ads consent choice, a cached copy of your profile and of the store’s catalogue for faster loading, downloaded pictures, and all guest-mode progress. This data never leaves your device.

2.6 Support requests

When you contact us from Settings → Help → Contact Us, or with the form on the Data Deletion page, we store the e-mail address you give (where our answer goes), the name and player code you give, the topic, your message, the App version and its language, and — in online mode — your account. We use a request only to answer it.

3. Information we do not collect

  • No photos, camera or microphone access. Avatars are chosen from a built-in gallery.
  • No contacts, no location (neither precise nor from GPS), no SMS or call logs.
  • No payment card or bank details. The App sells nothing for real money; if in-app purchases are added later, payment will be handled entirely by Google Play and this policy will be updated first.
  • No biometric data, no health data, no government identifiers.

The Android permissions the App uses are: Internet access; network state (to detect being offline); on Android 13 and later, permission to show notifications (asked only when you turn notifications on in Settings); vibration (for the vibration setting); and the Advertising ID permission used by the ads services. The ads and Google Play libraries also add their own standard permissions (such as keeping the device awake briefly to deliver a notification).

4. How we use information

  • To run the game: create and sign in to your account, match you with other players, run online matches and café sittings on our game server, settle results, and keep your progress in sync across devices.
  • Social features: friends, invitations, reminders, watching, gifts, blocking, chat and presence.
  • Competitions and rewards: the weekly and world rankings, the season pass, the elite challenge, check-ins, missions, achievements and chests, including paying the automatic weekly prizes to the top players.
  • Notifications you opted in to, such as friend requests, invitations and game announcements.
  • Advertising that keeps the App free.
  • Improving the App: understanding which features are used and fixing crashes.
  • Security and fair play: preventing cheating, modified apps, fraud and abuse; filtering chat; reviewing player reports; enforcing our Terms of Use, including muting, suspending or banning accounts.
  • Support: answering the requests you send us.
  • Legal obligations and responding to lawful requests.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for automated decisions with legal effects on you.

7. What other players can see

Because Domino Lux Stars is a multiplayer game, some of your information is visible to other players by design:

  • Your username, avatar and frame, level, country flag, popularity and your public statistics on your player card, at the table, in the café, in friends lists and in the rankings. If you hide your profile in Privacy controls, others see your card without your record.
  • Your online status and when you were last seen are visible to your friends, unless you hide them in Privacy controls.
  • When friends watch your match, the players at that table can see that they are watching.
  • Your player code is shown on your profile so friends can find you; anyone who knows the code can look up your card and send a request.
  • Messages, emojis, stickers and gifts you send are seen by the players at that table or in that café hall.

Your e-mail address is never shown to other players. You can block a player at any time (blocked players cannot send you requests or invitations, and their messages are hidden from you) and report a player from their player card — at the table too, by tapping their portrait — or a café message, for cheating, an offensive username or harassment.

8. Advertising

The App is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob and, through AdMob, by its mediation partner Unity Ads: rewarded video ads you choose to watch for in-game rewards (the button always says it is an ad and what it gives), occasional full-screen ads at the end of a match, and native ads on the round-results and match-results screens. Ad placements are controlled remotely by us and may change. Ads are limited to content suitable for teenagers.

  • To serve and measure ads, AdMob and its partners may use your device’s Advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, and information about your device and the ads you interact with.
  • Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and some U.S. states), the App shows a consent screen (Google User Messaging Platform) before personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy & ad consent.
  • You can also limit ad personalisation or reset your Advertising ID in your device settings. Learn how to control ad personalisation on your device.

For details on how Google uses data for advertising, read Google’s advertising technologies policy.

9. Analytics and crash reports

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the App is used (for example how long sessions last and how many matches are played). In online mode these events are linked to your random account identifier so that we can understand the game across your devices; they never carry your e-mail address or your username, and we look at them in aggregate. We use Firebase Crashlytics to detect and fix crashes; in online mode a crash report carries your random account identifier, so that we can match it to a support request you send us.

Read Firebase’s Privacy and Security overview for the retention periods and safeguards Google applies to this data.

10. Notifications

Notifications are off until you turn them on in Settings. With your permission, the App can notify you about friend requests, invitations, café openings you asked to be told about, and game announcements. Push notifications are delivered by Firebase Cloud Messaging using a device token that we store with your account, together with your language. You can turn notifications off at any time in the App’s Settings or in your device’s notification settings; the token is removed when you turn them off or sign out, and we keep at most your five most recent devices.

11. Security and anti-cheat

  • All communication between the App and our servers is encrypted in transit (TLS); the App refuses unencrypted connections.
  • The App checks on your device that it runs under its own signing certificate, and closes if it has been modified and re-signed. This check reads nothing about you and sends nothing anywhere.
  • Access to your data is protected by authentication and database access rules; the App carries no administrator key, and the game economy (coins, gems, tickets, results, rewards) is decided by our servers, never by the App alone.
  • When you are signed in and online, the App may obtain a token from the Google Play Integrity API and send it to our game server. The token tells us whether the App is genuine and whether the device appears unmodified. We receive Google’s verdict only, not your hardware identifiers. Modified apps or devices may be restricted from online play. Learn how the Play Integrity API works.
  • Accounts that cheat, exploit or abuse other players may be muted, suspended or banned; we keep the reason for the ban with the account.

No system is perfectly secure. If you believe your account has been compromised, contact us immediately.

Google Play Games

When you are signed in to an online account, the App starts Google Play Games Services, which may sign you in to Play Games automatically and show Google’s own welcome banner. The App reads only whether you are signed in to Play Games: it reads no Play Games profile, name, picture or player ID, and sends nothing from Play Games to our servers. Guests and offline play never start it. Google processes your Play Games data under Google’s Privacy Policy.

13. Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account yourself at any time from Settings → Account → Delete Account inside the App. This immediately and permanently erases your sign-in record (including your e-mail address), your profile, progress, items, friends, requests, blocks, gift bag, purchases, reports about you and notification tokens from our servers, and signs you out. A match in progress must end first.

You can also ask us with the form on the Data Deletion Request page or by e-mail. That page lists the full steps, and exactly what is deleted and what is kept.

Please note: deletion is permanent. Coins, gems, tickets, cosmetics and progress cannot be restored afterwards.

14. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you and receive a copy.
  • Correct it — your username, avatar, frame and country can be edited in the App, and the language in Settings.
  • Delete it (see above).
  • Object to, or restrict, certain processing, and withdraw consent at any time: ads consent from Settings → Privacy & ad consent, notifications from Settings or your device, ad personalisation from your device’s Google settings, presence, profile and watching from Privacy controls.
  • Data portability — receive your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Complain to your data protection authority. In the EEA, find your data protection authority.

California residents: we do not sell personal information. The use of your Advertising ID for personalised ads may count as “sharing” under California law; you can opt out through the in-app consent screen or your device settings as described in Advertising. We do not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

To exercise any right, e-mail userconplay@gmail.com from the Google address linked to your account, or include your player code. We respond within 30 days.

Where data leaves the EEA or UK, our providers rely on recognised safeguards such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses. By using the App you understand that your data may be processed in these locations.

If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it promptly.
